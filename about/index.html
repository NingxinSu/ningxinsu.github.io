About Me

Meet Ningxin Su (pronounced “Ning-shin Soo”, written as “苏宁馨” in Chinese characters) — a multi-faceted individual with a passion for the arts, sports, and knowledge. With experience conducting a large choir, drumming in a rock band, and teaching dance, Ningxin showcases a deep love for music and movement. As an accomplished long-distance runner, Ningxin is always eager to try new sports and physical challenges. Ningxin is also known for managing large-scale teams, bringing people together to create memorable experiences. In addition, Ningxin is an avid traveler, seeking out new museums, cultures, and natural wonders.

Ningxin leads NEBULIS Lab at HKUST(GZ). Her current research connects embodied AI and distributed systems, with an emphasis on robot manipulation and multi-arm collaboration. Building on her background in distributed and federated learning, she studies how embodied agents can act reliably and collaborate effectively in the physical world. She welcomes prospective PhD students, Research Assistants, and Interns interested in these directions to get in touch.

Whether on stage or in the lab, Ningxin strives to constantly learn, grow, and push herself to new heights.
